HPGL (Hewlett & Packard Graphics Language)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Exports the PCB for plotters and laser printers.
Only available for KiCad 9 and older. |br|
This output is what you get from the File/Plot menu in pcbnew. |br|
